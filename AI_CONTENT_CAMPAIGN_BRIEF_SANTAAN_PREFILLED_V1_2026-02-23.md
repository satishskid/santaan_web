## 4) Campaign decision table (Scale / Fix / Pause)

### 4.1 Rulebook (default)
- **Scale** if all are true:
  - conversion rate >= center minimum
  - attribution >= 95%
  - pending >24h within threshold
  - CPP <= 1.1x rolling 4-week median CPP for same center/channel
- **Fix** if any are true:
  - high leads but low qualified ratio
  - high qualified but low converted
  - attribution between 85% and 94%
- **Pause** if all are true for 2 consecutive weeks:
  - spend high vs center average
  - conversion rate below threshold
  - no quality lift after at least 2 creative/audience fixes
