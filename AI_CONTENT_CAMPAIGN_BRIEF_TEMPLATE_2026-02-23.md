## 9) Weekly CEO Review Protocol (30 min)
1. Confirm data integrity first (attribution + input compliance).
2. Review funnel leaks (delay, attribution, counseling, trust).
3. Approve top 3 campaign actions (scale/fix/pause).
4. Approve weekly content slate tied to real objections.
5. Assign owners with deadlines; no unowned action items.

## 10) AI Prompt Pack (Use with caution + human review)

### 10.1 Prompt: Weekly insight synthesis
"Using this CRM weekly export, identify top intent clusters by center, top objections, leakage points, and 5 campaign actions (scale/fix/pause). Output concise tables and owner actions. Do not invent clinical claims."

### 10.2 Prompt: Patient blog ideation
"From these top patient objections and search intents, generate 8 blog topic ideas with target keyword, audience stage, and CTA. Avoid claiming exact success rates or fixed IVF costs."

### 10.3 Prompt: Social reel scripting
"Create 10 short reel scripts (30-45 sec) based on this week’s top objections. Include hook, myth-bust, empathetic close, and consult CTA. Keep medically safe language."

### 10.4 Prompt: Campaign fix plan
"For campaigns marked FIX, propose copy angle changes, audience refinement, landing page updates, and 1-week experiment plan with pass/fail metrics."
