## 4) Guided CEO Setup (Day-by-Day)

### Day 0 (Friday, 2026-02-20) - Instrumentation Lock
1. Open `/admin/dashboard` -> `Analytics` and `CEO Command`.
2. Confirm team owners:
   - Marketing Manager: UTM compliance
   - CRM Lead: callback SLA
   - Counseling Lead: qualified->converted closure
3. Approve UTM taxonomy (mandatory):
   - `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content`, `utm_term`, center landing path
4. Approve center landing URLs:
   - `/ivf-clinic-bhubaneswar`
   - `/ivf-clinic-berhampur`
   - `/ivf-clinic-bangalore-aecs-layout`

### Day 1 (Saturday, 2026-02-21) - Asset Mapping
1. Map each active asset to one landing URL + one UTM campaign.
2. Publish link sheet with owner and launch date.
3. Ensure TV/hoarding QR routes are center-specific.

### Day 2 (Sunday, 2026-02-22) - Dry Run
1. Submit test leads from each campaign link.
2. Validate in CRM list:
   - source, medium, campaign, landing path populated
   - status initialized as `new`
3. Fix any null attribution before launch.

### Day 3 (Monday, 2026-02-23) - Launch
1. Start all approved campaigns.
2. CEO command review at end of day:
   - total leads per center
   - pending >24h
   - untagged lead count

### Day 4 (Tuesday, 2026-02-24) - Speed Enforcement
1. CRM lead executes callback sweep for all `new` and `contacted`.
2. Counseling lead audits qualified pipeline and books consult slots.
3. CEO checks that no center has pending >24h above threshold.

### Day 5 (Wednesday, 2026-02-25) - Midweek Budget Shift
1. In `Analytics`, identify top two campaigns by conversion and high-intent mix.
2. Shift 10-15% budget from weak assets to winners.
3. Enforce `Not Tagged = 0` from this day onward.

### Day 6 (Thursday, 2026-02-26) - Conversion Deep Dive
1. CEO + Counseling lead review all qualified-but-not-converted leads.
2. Categorize drop reasons:
   - pricing concern
   - timing concern
   - trust concern
   - follow-up delay
3. Deploy one fix per reason same day.

### Day 7 (Friday, 2026-02-27) - Center Performance Review
1. Compare Bhubaneswar, Berhampur, Bangalore on:
   - leads
   - conversion
   - stale pending
2. If any center is 30% below best center conversion, assign corrective plan owner.

### Day 8 (Saturday, 2026-02-28) - Winback Day
1. Trigger winback for lost leads created in last 14 days.
2. Offer priority callback/slot.

### Day 9 (Sunday, 2026-03-01) - Weekly Close
1. Freeze week metrics by center.
2. Record target vs actual.
3. Approve Week-2 targets:
   - keep winning campaigns
   - drop low-efficiency assets
