# Santaan Agency NeoDove SLA Charter
Date: March 11, 2026

Purpose: Define the service level agreement for the agency managing NeoDove operations and telecaller performance within Santaan's operating rules.

## 1. Scope
The agency will manage NeoDove operations under Santaan's system design and reporting rules.

Agency is responsible for:
- campaign to NeoDove mapping
- number to campaign mapping
- daily queue hygiene review
- stale lead monitoring
- webhook and workflow health monitoring
- telecaller operational oversight
- daily CRM reporting discipline

Agency is not authorized to change without Santaan approval:
- stage taxonomy
- lost reason taxonomy
- counselor handoff rules
- core campaign architecture
- source bucket structure

## 2. Operating principle
- Santaan owns architecture and taxonomy.
- Agency operates NeoDove within approved rules.
- Telecaller team executes inside NeoDove.
- Santaan CRM remains the final reporting and leadership system.

## 3. Daily SLA
### A. Attribution SLA
- 100% active paid campaigns mapped to the correct NeoDove campaign
- 100% paid call source buckets mapped to the correct number
- 100% website and WhatsApp source routes mapped to an approved campaign bucket
- no paid campaign allowed to run without traceable source mapping

### B. Reporting SLA
- by 11:00 AM IST:
  - spend updated in Santaan CRM
  - campaign status updated
  - UTM and campaign hygiene verified

- by 11:30 AM IST:
  - NeoDove queue health reviewed
  - stale lead list reviewed
  - missed-call queue reviewed

- by 6:00 PM IST:
  - second queue review completed
  - campaign routing issues escalated
  - high-priority misses documented

### C. Telecalling oversight SLA
Agency must ensure the telecalling team maintains:
- hot leads contacted within 10 minutes
- all new leads contacted within 2 hours
- no qualified lead without same-day counselor handoff
- no lost lead without lost reason
- no stale OPEN lead greater than 2 hours without documented reason

### D. Integration SLA
- webhook failure reviewed same day
- workflow failure escalated within 2 hours
- routing or campaign mapping issue corrected same day

## 4. Weekly SLA
Agency must submit a weekly operating summary containing:
- attribution accuracy review
- stale lead leakage review
- missed-call recovery performance
- telecaller SLA compliance summary
- campaign to qualified lead trend
- recommended scale, pause, and fix actions

## 5. KPI scorecard
Agency will be reviewed on:
- mapping completeness: 25%
- reporting timeliness: 15%
- telecaller SLA compliance: 25%
- stale lead control: 15%
- attribution accuracy: 10%
- escalation discipline: 10%

## 6. Red-flag conditions
Immediate escalation required if:
- any paid campaign runs without NeoDove mapping
- any webhook fails for more than 2 hours
- stale new leads exceed threshold without action
- qualified leads are not handed off same day
- campaign-wise lead numbers are reported without attribution basis

## 7. Telecaller operating requirements
For every touched lead, telecaller must update:
- status
- last contact time
- call outcome
- next follow-up time
- lost reason if lost
- qualified handoff note if qualified

Required stages:
- OPEN
- CONTACTED
- QUALIFIED
- CONSULT_BOOKED
- CONVERTED
- LOST

Required lost reasons:
- price
- not_ready
- family_discussion
- competitor
- no_response
- outside_city
- medical_deferral
- duplicate_invalid

## 8. Reporting rule for call campaigns
Until campaign-wise call attribution is fully preserved inside NeoDove:
- agency may report spend, impressions, and clicks accurately
- exact campaign-wise call leads must not be guessed
- where attribution is not preserved, note:
  "Campaign-wise call attribution unavailable; center-level truth only"

## 9. Evidence and audit
Agency must be able to produce on request:
- NeoDove campaign master
- number to campaign mapping
- telecaller ownership list
- stale lead review log
- workflow and webhook status proof
- daily CRM update evidence

## 10. Ownership matrix
### Santaan
- architecture
- taxonomy
- counselor rules
- CRM reporting rules
- final escalation decisions

### Agency
- NeoDove operations
- queue monitoring
- campaign hygiene
- telecaller operational discipline
- daily reporting compliance

### Telecalling lead
- daily execution quality
- queue balancing
- stale lead resolution
- counselor handoff quality

## 11. Compliance outcome
If agency meets this SLA:
- CEO gets trustworthy campaign and telecalling visibility
- telecaller operations become measurable
- agency reporting becomes audit-proof
- Santaan CRM becomes a real growth operating system

If agency does not meet this SLA:
- attribution breaks
- lead reporting weakens
- CEO decisions are distorted
- agency performance review is downgraded
