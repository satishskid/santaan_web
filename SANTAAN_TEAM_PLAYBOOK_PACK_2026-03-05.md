## 3) Daily Operating Sequence (Simple)

1. Data in by teams (morning to evening windows).  
2. Content topics picked from real CRM signals.  
3. Publish blog + social assets with mandatory tags/UTMs.  
4. End-of-day CRM reconciliation.  
5. CEO sees one clean action snapshot, not raw noise.

## 4) Non-Negotiables

- No UTM = unattributed spend.
- No reason code = incomplete lead update.
- No CTA = incomplete content.
- No CRM closure by day-end = task not done.

## 5) Weekly Governance Rhythm

- Monday: priority topics + campaign action plan.
- Wednesday: mid-week SLA and leakage review.
- Saturday: keep/kill/scale decisions by CEO.
