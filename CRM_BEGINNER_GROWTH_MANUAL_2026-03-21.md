# Santaan CRM Beginner Growth Manual (2026-03-21)

This is a simple, non‑technical guide to how the Santaan CRM system works and why each part matters.

## 1) What this system is
The CRM is the daily operating system for Santaan growth. It connects:
- Leads from ads, website, calls, WhatsApp, and IVR
- Team follow‑ups and status updates
- Campaign performance and spend
- Daily accountability (Today, Workboard, Daily Command)

If data is updated here, leadership can take faster, better decisions.

## 2) Key terms in simple language

**Lead**
Someone who showed interest in Santaan (form, call, WhatsApp, etc.).

**Qualified lead**
Someone who is genuinely ready for consultation or follow‑up.

**Converted lead**
Someone who becomes a patient.

**UTM**
A tracking code added to links so we know which ad or post created the lead.  
Example: `utm_source=meta&utm_campaign=ivf_bhubaneswar_q2`

**Meta**
Facebook and Instagram ads.

**GA4 (Google Analytics 4)**
Google’s tool that tells us how many people visited our site and which pages they viewed.

**Search Console**
Google’s tool that shows what people search for on Google before reaching our site.

**IVR**
Interactive voice response system (call system) that collects leads by phone.

**NeoDove**
The telecalling system we use for call tracking and outcomes.

**Spend**
How much money we spent on ads. This must be logged daily.

**Workboard**
Daily tasks that each role must complete and close.

## 3) Why UTM tracking matters
UTM helps answer:
- Which ad brought the lead?
- Which campaign is working?
- Where should we scale and where to pause?

If UTM is missing, we lose visibility and waste budget.

## 4) Why Meta and Google feedback matters

**Meta feedback (Facebook/Instagram)**
- Shows ad reach, clicks, leads, and cost.
- Helps decide scale/pause.

**Google feedback (GA4 + Search Console)**
- Shows which pages and topics people want.
- Helps content team write better posts.

Both are critical for better decisions.

## 5) How leads move in CRM

1. New lead comes in (Meta, website, call, WhatsApp, IVR).
2. Telecaller/counselor calls the lead.
3. Status is updated:
   - New → Contacted → Qualified → Converted
4. Next follow‑up is scheduled.
5. Daily work is logged in Workboard.

If the status is not updated, the system becomes blind.

## 6) Daily workflow for all staff

**Morning**
- Open **Today**
- Follow the checklist

**Day**
- Do role work from your assigned tabs

**Evening**
- Close tasks in **Workboard**
- Ensure no active lead is missing a next follow‑up

## 7) Role‑wise focus (beginner level)

**Content Manager**
- Use Analytics to pick topics and keywords
- Fill Meta Launch drafts with hook + copy + brief
- Share drafts for approval

**Performance / Ads**
- Log daily spend
- Use Meta Launch to create campaigns
- Update status as launched

**Telecaller / IVR Lead**
- Call hot and due follow‑ups first
- Update lead status and next follow‑up
- Keep NeoDove sync clean

**Counselor**
- Focus on qualified leads
- Update outcomes after every call

**Field Team**
- Log offline activity with tracking handles

**CEO / CRM Ops**
- Check Today + Analytics + Daily Command
- Decide 3–5 priorities only
- Close day and unblock team

## 8) Common mistakes to avoid
- Not updating lead status
- Forgetting next follow‑up time
- Logging spend late
- Missing UTMs in links
- Closing the day without Workboard updates

## 9) How to know if the system is healthy
You should see:
- Few stale leads
- High number of follow‑ups done daily
- Spend updated every day
- Meta/Google reports showing real performance
- Workboard tasks mostly “done” by evening

## 10) Golden rule (repeat daily)
CRM not updated = work not counted.

This is the single most important rule for team success.
