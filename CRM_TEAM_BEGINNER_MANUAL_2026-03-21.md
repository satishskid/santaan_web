# Santaan CRM Beginner Manual (2026-03-21)

This guide is for non‑technical Santaan team members. It explains what to do every day, in simple steps, without marketing jargon.

**Purpose**
The CRM is your daily work guide. It shows what to do in the morning, what to work on during the day, and how to close in the evening. If everyone updates their tasks here, leadership sees the real picture and can help faster.

**Login**
1. Open the CRM link shared by admin.
2. Enter your assigned PIN.
3. If your PIN does not work, ask admin to reset it.

**Golden Rules**
- Always update the lead status after you speak to someone.
- Always set a next follow‑up time for active leads.
- If you do any work, log it in CRM the same day.
- Don’t guess. Use the suggested tasks and buttons in your role view.

**Daily Routine (All Roles)**
1. Morning
Check the **Today** panel. It tells you exactly what to do first.
2. Day Work
Open your role tab and do the tasks listed there.
3. Evening
Open **Workboard** or **Daily Command** and mark what you completed.

**Your Main Screens**
- **Today**: Your daily checklist and quick buttons.
- **Workboard**: All tasks for the day, grouped by time.
- **Daily Command**: Leadership view of all team execution.
- **Contacts**: All leads and their status.
- **Follow‑ups**: People who need action within 24 hours.
- **Hot Leads**: Highest priority leads.
- **Meta Launch**: Content + ads planning (content and performance roles).
- **Analytics**: Trends and recommendations (leadership, content, performance).
- **NeoDove Ops**: Telecalling queue and sync health (telecaller manager).

**How to Update a Lead**
1. Open **Contacts** or **Follow‑ups**.
2. Click a lead.
3. Update:
   - Status
   - Notes
   - Next follow‑up time
4. Save.

If a lead is qualified, make sure you select **Qualified** status.
If a lead is converted to patient, select **Converted** status.

**Role‑Wise Instructions**

**CEO / CRM Ops**
1. Morning: Open **Today** and **Analytics**.
2. Decide only 3–5 key actions for the day.
3. Share those decisions with the team.
4. Evening: Open **Workboard** and close the day.

**Performance Marketer / Ads**
1. Morning: Open **Spend** and log yesterday’s spend.
2. Open **Meta Launch** to see approved draft packs.
3. Create or update campaigns in Meta using the provided Ads Manager link.
4. Evening: Update Workboard and mark what launched.

**Content Manager**
1. Morning: Open **Analytics** and see the suggested topics and keywords.
2. Open **Meta Launch** and fill:
   - Hook
   - Primary text
   - Headline
   - Creative brief
3. Mark the draft as ready for approval.
4. Evening: Log what was published and any learnings in Workboard.

**Telecaller Manager / IVR**
1. Morning: Open **NeoDove Ops** and **Hot Leads**.
2. Assign or call high‑priority leads first.
3. Clear all follow‑ups due in 24 hours.
4. Evening: Ensure NeoDove sync is clean and update Workboard.

**Counselor**
1. Open **Qualified** or **Follow‑ups**.
2. Call or counsel the lead.
3. Update status and next follow‑up time.
4. Evening: Ensure no active lead is missing next action.

**Field Team**
1. Log field activity in **Ops Inputs**.
2. Always add tracking handles (QR, call number, WhatsApp number).
3. Close tasks in Workboard by evening.

**Meta Launch (For Content + Ads Team)**
This replaces long WhatsApp handoffs.
1. Content fills draft with hooks, keywords, and creative brief.
2. Performance approves and opens Ads Manager link.
3. Performance updates status to **Launched** after creating the campaign.

**Common Problems**
- “I can’t see my tab.”
  Ask admin to check your role.
- “My lead disappeared.”
  Check the status filter or search.
- “I forgot to log something.”
  Add it before day end in Workboard or update the lead note.

**Success Checklist**
- Every lead has a status.
- Every active lead has a next follow‑up time.
- Every role closes tasks in Workboard.
- Daily decisions are written once and followed by execution.

If you follow this routine daily, the CRM becomes easy and leadership can help faster.
