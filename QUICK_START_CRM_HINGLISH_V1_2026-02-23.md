# Santaan CRM Quick Start (Hinglish) - 1 Page

Date: 23 Feb 2026
Use for: Telecaller, Field Team, Counselor, Agency, CEO

## 1) Login kaise karein
1. Open karo: `/login`
2. Email + password daalo
3. Login ke baad jao: `/admin/dashboard`

Agar login issue aaye:
- email spelling check karo
- password check karo
- CRM Ops Admin se reset lo

---

## 2) Golden Rule (sab ke liye)
**CRM me update nahi hua = kaam done nahi maana jayega.**

Har task me 3 cheezein zaroor daalo:
1. Status (`pending / in_progress / done / blocked`)
2. Note (kya kiya)
3. Next owner (agar pending hai)

---

## 3) Daily kaam role-wise

### A) Telecaller / IVR Team
- `All Contacts` + `Hot Leads` kholna
- Lead ko touch karte hi status update karo
- `new -> contacted -> qualified -> converted/lost`
- Hot lead ko 10 min ke andar call
- New lead ko 2 hour ke andar first touch
- Workboard me cycle note daalo

### B) Counselor
- Qualified leads ka same-day follow-up
- Consult booking / closure status update
- Lost hua to reason note karo
- Workboard task `done` mark karo

### C) Field Team
- `Ops Inputs -> Field Team` me same-day entry
- Must fill: center, activity type, location, owner, utm_campaign
- QR/call/WhatsApp me se at least 1 tracking handle mandatory
- Workboard task close karo

### D) Agency Team
- `Ops Inputs -> Agency` by 11:00 AM daily
- Campaign spend + UTM details mandatory
- 3:00 PM tak underperformers flag karo
- TV active hai to `Ops Inputs -> TV Ads` fill karo
- Workboard note me correction plan likho

### E) CEO / CRM Ops
- Morning: Workboard summary + CEO Command check
- Pending/blocked items pe owner assign karo
- Evening: unresolved blockers close karo

---

## 4) Status ka simple meaning
- `pending` = start nahi hua
- `in_progress` = kaam chal raha hai
- `done` = complete ho gaya
- `blocked` = issue hai, reason + owner likhna compulsory

---

## 5) End-of-day checklist (sab roles)
- [ ] Aaj ke sab assigned Workboard tasks update hue
- [ ] Notes clear hain (kya kiya + next step)
- [ ] Contacts status fresh hai
- [ ] Pending >24h leads identify hue
- [ ] Blocked items owner ke saath tagged hain

---

## 6) Common mistakes avoid karo
1. Blank note mat chhodo
2. Wrong center/utm naming mat daalo
3. End me bulk update mat karo (real-time update karo)
4. Lost lead without reason mat close karo

---

## 7) Escalation
Agar koi bhi item `blocked` hai:
1. Reason likho
2. Kaun fix karega likho
3. Due time likho
4. CRM Ops/Lead ko same day ping karo

**No owner + no due time = no closure.**
