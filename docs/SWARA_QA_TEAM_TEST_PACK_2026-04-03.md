## Purpose

This QA round checks whether Swara feels like a real, patient-facing first-contact assistant for Santaan Fertility Centre.

We are not only testing lead capture.
We are testing whether a caller feels heard, attended to, and gently guided toward a helpful next step.

## How To Test

1. Accept the Edesy invite and log in.
2. Open Test Hub.
3. Select agent: `Swara - Santaan Odia`.
4. Enter your own mobile number.
5. Click `Test Call`.
6. Perform only your assigned scenario.
7. Speak naturally like a real caller.
8. Fill the evaluation form immediately after the call.

## Important Rules

- Use the dashboard / Test Hub only.
- Do not call the public number directly yet.
- Do only your assigned scenario in the first call.
- If your scenario includes pauses, pause where written.
- If your scenario includes interruption, interrupt while the bot is speaking.
- Note exact awkward phrases if any.
- Note if the bot cuts you off.
- Note if Turns or Transcript are missing in Edesy.
- Do not overthink pronunciation or act dramatically. Speak normally like a real caller.
