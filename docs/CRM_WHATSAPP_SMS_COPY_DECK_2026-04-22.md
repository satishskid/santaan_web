# Santaan CRM WhatsApp + SMS Copy Deck

Date: 2026-04-22
Purpose: Short-message follow-up copy for warm leads who need education, excitement, and one clean CTA click.

## Core Rule
- One message, one idea, one link.
- Do not stack multiple CTAs in the same message.
- Use curiosity + clarity, not long explanations.
- The URL should finish the sentence naturally.
- For WhatsApp, keep most first-touch messages under 220 characters.
- For SMS, keep most messages under 140-160 characters if possible.

## URL Map
- `https://santaan.in/fertility-guides`
  Best for: general warm leads who are exploring and not yet ready to book.
- `https://santaan.in/fertility-conditions`
  Best for: leads who mention PCOS, thyroid, low sperm count, irregular cycles, or unexplained delay.
- `https://santaan.in/know-your-score`
  Best for: interactive CTA, curiosity CTA, self-check CTA.
- `https://santaan.in/fertility-tips`
  Best for: soft nurture, newsletter, low-pressure education follow-up.
- `https://santaan.in/at-home-fertility-testing`
  Best for: higher-intent leads ready to take the next step.

## Best Use By Lead Type
- Cold but curious: `fertility-guides`
- Warm and researching symptoms or causes: `fertility-conditions`
- Warm and engaged, needs a next step: `know-your-score`
- Warm but not ready to speak/book: `fertility-tips`
- Hot and ready: `at-home-fertility-testing`

## Do / Don’t

### Do
- Personalize the opener with first name if available.
- Match the link to the concern already expressed by the lead.
- Use “start here”, “check”, “explore”, or “know your score”.
- Keep the emotional promise light and credible.

### Don’t
- Do not send 2-3 links in one message.
- Do not explain IVF, IUI, PCOS, and testing all in the same text.
- Do not sound clinical too early.
- Do not use “book now” on first warm follow-up unless intent is already high.
